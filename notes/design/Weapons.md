# Weapons

- Weapons should be limited in number, with just a single weapon per archetype (e.g. Hades or Silksong).
- Additional gear can modify and add onto archetypes to create more unique builds
- Avoid tying weapon moveset to other properties
  - Example: Silksong ties special binds and tool slots to particular movesets, but this causes issues with buildcrafting by tying unrelated properties together.
